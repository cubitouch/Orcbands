Meet our first orc

Use Crash site to inspect the character in a procedurally generated starting valley, beside our first zeppelin wreck and tiny orc shelter. This is an exploratory browser diorama, not the Unity game.

This is actual polygon geometry loaded from the original OBJ mesh and materials—not AI concept art or a prerecorded video.

The preview animates rigid body sections (arms and legs) for testing proportions and readability. It is not a skinned or verified Unity Humanoid yet. Clothing, face, topology, proper rigging, and walk/chop cycles all need further refinement.
