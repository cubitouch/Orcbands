Pine 01, Timber Hut 01 & the forest grove

Our first environment asset is an original low-poly evergreen: nine-sided tapered trunk, four staggered faceted foliage tiers, and seven deliberately restrained material colors. Switch to Forest grove to judge its readability at a typical elevated RTS angle. The forest camp combines 244 instances of this preserved mesh with a winding dirt path, varied ground cover, Timber Hut 01 berry patches, a small woodcutting site, a lively campfire and four trail torches, with gently shifting firelight, drifting smoke and occasional sparks. Fog of war softly conceals woodland beyond the hut and firelight; toggle it off to inspect the full forest.

Inspect Resources for full, partly picked and empty berry bushes, plus the preserved standing pine, stump, felled trunk, cut logs and wood chips. Each resource state can be downloaded as an OBJ with embedded vertex colors. These are static art states for future harvesting.

The static OBJ is about 4.58 units high and 2.72 units wide (assuming 1 unit = 1 meter). No wind animation, Unity prefab, LODs, or colliders yet. Both geometry and materials are generated deterministically.
