A reusable tree, not a concept illustration

Our first environment asset is an original low-poly evergreen: nine-sided tapered trunk, four staggered faceted foliage tiers, and seven deliberately restrained material colors. Switch to Small grove to judge its readability at a typical elevated RTS angle. The grove uses seven instances of this same real mesh, not seven hand-authored variants.

The static OBJ is about 4.58 units high and 2.72 units wide (assuming 1 unit = 1 meter). No wind animation, Unity prefab, LODs, or colliders yet. Both geometry and materials are generated deterministically.

Download tree OBJ Download MTL colors View mesh generator

For Unity, put the OBJ and MTL in the same folder. Unity can import the mesh natively; verify scale and normals and reconnect materials if needed. This browser Asset Lab is not a Unity build.

The earlier procedural orc model experiments have been retired in favor of using proper licensed characters later. No third-party characters are bundled here.
